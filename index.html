The first waves are a gentle warm-up; the Swarm sharpens up every wave. Stick forward dives, like a flight sim; press I (or controller Back) to swap. Hold a target in your sights to lock missiles. Every third wave is an asteroid storm. Shoot down a Warden carrying a captured fighter and that pilot joins you as a wingman.
